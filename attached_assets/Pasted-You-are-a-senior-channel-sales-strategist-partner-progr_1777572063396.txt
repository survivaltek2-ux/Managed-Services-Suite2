You are a senior channel sales strategist, partner program architect, and B2B marketing copywriter.

Create a complete **Referral Program** for **Siebert Services**, a **B2B MSP and technology reseller**. Siebert Services sells business technology solutions such as internet, connectivity, VoIP/UCaaS, managed IT services, Microsoft 365, cybersecurity, physical security, backup/disaster recovery, and related telecom/IT solutions.

Important context:
- Siebert Services wants to keep its main **Partner Program** focused on businesses, agents, MSPs, consultants, and reseller-type organizations.
- Consumers and individuals should **not** be treated as full channel partners.
- Instead, Siebert Services wants a separate **Referral Program** for individuals, advocates, customers, and non-reseller contacts who refer business opportunities.
- The program should be simple, professional, scalable, and easy to manage operationally.

Your task is to design the referral program and produce practical deliverables.

Please provide the output in clearly labeled sections with concise but useful detail.

Include the following:

1. **Program Overview**
   - Program purpose
   - Target audience
   - How it differs from the full partner program
   - Key benefits to Siebert Services and participants

2. **Program Naming Ideas**
   - Provide 10 name options
   - Include a recommendation for the best option
   - Explain why it fits the brand

3. **Eligibility Rules**
   - Who can join
   - Who should not join
   - What activities are allowed and not allowed

4. **Referral Reward Structure**
   - Provide 3 payout model options:
     - flat fee
     - tiered reward
     - percentage-based
   - Recommend the best model for launch
   - Include sample payout ranges for services like internet, VoIP, managed IT, and multi-location opportunities

5. **Lead Qualification Rules**
   - Define what makes a lead valid
   - Define duplicate lead policy
   - Define when a referral becomes payable
   - Include fraud prevention rules and self-referral restrictions

6. **Operational Workflow**
   - Step-by-step lifecycle from signup to payout
   - Suggested lead statuses
   - Suggested payout statuses
   - Internal team workflow recommendations

7. **Program Terms and Conditions**
   - Draft simple but professional terms
   - Cover payment timing, taxes, duplicate submissions, prohibited behavior, marketing restrictions, and right to modify/terminate the program

8. **Website Copy**
   - Landing page headline
   - Subheadline
   - Benefits section
   - How it works section
   - FAQ section
   - CTA button text
   - Signup form fields
   - Referral submission form fields

9. **Email Templates**
   - Welcome email for new referrers
   - Referral received confirmation email
   - Qualified referral update email
   - Payout notification email

10. **Internal Policy Recommendations**
    - Approval standards
    - Response SLA
    - Payout review process
    - Compliance and documentation checklist

11. **MVP Launch Plan**
    - Recommend the simplest version to launch in 30 days
    - Include systems/processes Siebert Services would need
    - Include key risks and how to mitigate them

12. **Final Recommendation**
    - Summarize the best structure for Siebert Services
    - Make the recommendation practical, conservative, and scalable

Tone and style requirements:
- Professional
- B2B-focused
- Operationally realistic
- Clear and not overly legalistic
- Designed for a company that wants to scale without creating admin chaos

Where useful, present information in bullet points or tables.
